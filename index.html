Islamabad,The Beautiful City

Islamabad, the capital city of Pakistan,
is a uniquely planned metropolis nestled against the scenic backdrop of the Margalla Hills. Designed by Greek architect Constantinos A. Doxiadis in the 1960s, the city harmonizes modern urban planning, wide tree-lined boulevards, and abundant green spaces with a calm, peaceful atmosphere. Iconic landmarks like the striking Faisal Mosque and the petal-shaped Pakistan Monument highlight its architectural elegance, while popular spots like Trail 3 in the Margalla Hills and F-9 Park offer a seamless blend of city life and nature. Often celebrated as one of the world's most visually appealing capitals, Islamabad stands out as a clean, green, and serene sanctuary.
